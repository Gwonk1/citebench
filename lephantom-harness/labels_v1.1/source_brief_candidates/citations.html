LePhantomCite train split: citation and pincite errors in the source briefs

Companion to the quotation list (adopted in dataset v1.1) and the unreviewed test-split candidates. Train half only (910 excerpts of LePhantomCite v1.1). These are the deterministic checker’s remaining “wrong” calls on citations and pin pages that match no gold span. The first two sections can be confirmed from the reporter in a minute each; the last two are weaker signals we cannot verify without a human read, listed so nothing is hidden. Twelve further unmatched findings were our own scoring artefacts (the checker caught a planted error but reported it on the citation rather than the case name) and are not listed. Prepared 2026-10-19 by Graham Syfert, syfert.com; syfert@gmail.com.
