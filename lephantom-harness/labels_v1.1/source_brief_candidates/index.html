Unmarked quotation errors in the source briefs of LePhantomCite

LePhantomCite is the benchmark in “Who Checks the Citations? Benchmarking Legal Hallucination Detection” (Liu, Stammbach & Henderson, Princeton, 2026; dataset ai-law-society-lab/Legal_Phantom_Citation). It plants citation errors into excerpts of real filed briefs and scores a system on the planted errors. The source briefs are treated as clean. We found, while running Syfert’s deterministic check_brief tool over the 910-excerpt train half (the 390-excerpt test half was not read), that the briefs themselves misquote the cases they cite in the rows below. Each quotation is attributed by the brief to the cited case, differs from the opinion without brackets or ellipsis, and sits in no gold span, so a checker that reports it is scored as a false positive. External check: CourtListener v4 search with the phrase restricted to the cited cluster, run with the brief’s wording and with the opinion’s wording (expected 0 and 1 hits). Opinion text as held by syfert.com (CourtListener / Caselaw Access Project copy). Three rows were confirmed by hand; two quotations not found in the opinion at all are listed but not yet reviewed. Prepared 2026-10-19 by Graham Syfert, syfert.com; corrections welcome (syfert@gmail.com). A companion page lists unreviewed candidates from the test split, generated without anyone here reading them, and a train-half list of citation and pincite candidates with its unreviewed test-split counterpart, and a partial, model-judged list of content-misrepresentation candidates. Finally, the sentences a frontier model with these tools flagged that the gold does not mark: train subset, hand-reviewed and test split, unreviewed.

Update, 19 Oct 2026: the authors reviewed this list the same day and released dataset v1.1: 39 of these quotations are now labelled optional (neither rewarded nor penalized), r076 and r184 were added as scored misquotes, r263 was corrected as an OCR error in the excerpt text, and r160 was not adopted. Every change is listed in the dataset’s updates/source_brief_errors.jsonl. The test half, which they had already audited, carried 22 optional spans since v1.0.
